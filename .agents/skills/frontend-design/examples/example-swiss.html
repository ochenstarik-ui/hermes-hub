§ 01 The Proposition

Wall text, 340 cm
Galerie 2, east wall
Set in Archivo Medium

The grid divides space. The designer decides what it carries. Typography can be loud enough to replace illustration — order strict enough to replace decoration.

Between 1950 and 1980, Basel and Zurich turned a teaching method into an international language. Josef Müller-Brockmann aligned a Beethoven poster to a grid and Concert Hall audiences noticed the poster before the concert. Emil Ruder set the measure of a page the way a composer sets tempo.

Ordnung assembles the evidence: the posters that argued, the journals that codified, the teaching materials that spread the method from the Allgemeine Gewerbeschule to design schools on four continents — and the concrete paintings that proved the same rules without a single letter.
