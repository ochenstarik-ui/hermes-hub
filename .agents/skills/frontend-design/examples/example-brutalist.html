Tide Marks

Mira Okafor

Format LP · CD · Cassette · Digital   Edition First press: 500 (black), 200 (clear w/ red)   Cat. № CST 142

Recorded over eighteen months in a converted boat-shed on the Saint Lawrence, Tide Marks is the most expansive record Mira has made — eight pieces that move between percussion, voice, and the sounds of the river itself. Mastered by Harris Newman at Grey Market. Sleeve design by Constellation's in-house studio.
